I am currently a band member of ToTheMax, which is comprised of colleagues working at the Princess Maxima Center. We have played in retreats and events hosted by the PMC as well as our own fundraising concerts. We play cover songs from various different music periods with a bigger focus on music from the 2000s and 2010s.
